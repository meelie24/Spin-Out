# Research Decision — Reality Ping Salience and Priority

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Category:** Behavioral interventions / notification salience

## Problem
Displaying a personalized pop-up does not guarantee attention, recall, limit adherence or durable behavior change. Prior synthetic users likewise remembered concrete real-life money context more often than generic observations.

## Evidence reviewed
- responsible-gambling pop-up systematic review/meta-analysis
- 2025 personalized-feedback intervention meta-analysis
- randomized informative vs self-appraisal pop-up work
- personalized loss feedback / limit adherence experiments
- large real-world enhanced-pop-up study
- real-world personalized behavioral feedback
- precommitment/limit research
- 2025 safer-gambling goal-setting/norms messaging feasibility work
- JITAI receptivity/intervention-fatigue research

## What repeats
1. Pop-ups can affect behavior/cognition, but effects vary.
2. Personalization alone is insufficient.
3. Recall is a bottleneck; some studies found about half could not recall the message.
4. Self-appraisal can outperform purely informative copy for recall in some settings.
5. Repeated interruption can irritate.
6. Precommitment matters because the decision was made before the high-arousal moment.
7. Timing and salience matter as much as content.
8. Recent personalized-feedback meta-analysis finds small/nonsignificant immediate effects overall and somewhat better follow-up effects.

## Spin Out decision
Preserve this hierarchy:
1. user-defined stopping limits / acute dangerous escalation
2. acute chase behavior
3. other high-salience distortions
4. concrete real-life context when urgency is comparable
5. generic observations

Do not make every money message “strong”.

Keep one foreground intervention, cooldowns, family suppression, intentional silence, X-Ray for specific cognitive moments, and strong two-choice surfaces only for genuinely strong moments.

## Message design
Normal Ping: one dominant sentence, one supporting line only when useful.  
Strong intervention: connect directly to earlier stop decision/current behavior.  
Continue stays neutral. I’m done stays user voice.  
Sensitive context must be fresh, user-confirmed and relevant.

## Success criteria
- limit/chase events still outrank ledger/context
- contextual same-urgency message outranks generic observation
- foreground frequency does not increase
- cooldown/family suppression intact
- recall improves without forced dwell timers
- irritation/creepiness do not increase
- exact money data only used when confirmed/relevant
- synthetic results separate normal/contextual/strong recall

## Sources
