# Research Decision — Reality Ping Salience and Personal Context

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Category:** Behavioral intervention / notification salience

## Problem
Ordinary Pings are less memorable than specific real-life money Pings. Too many generic interruptions can also irritate users and teach them to dismiss the surface.

## Evidence
Responsible-gambling studies repeatedly find:
- self-appraisal messages are often recalled better than purely informative messages
- successive popups suffer recall decay
- frequent popups can be irritating and disruptive
- personalized behavioral feedback can alter behavior for some users, but effects are not universal
- real-world enhanced messages have outperformed simple generic messages in cessation rates, though absolute effects remain modest
- personalized feedback without unnecessary normative comparison can be useful

In one controlled trial, 77% recalled at least one self-appraisal popup vs 35% for informative messages; recall beyond the first message dropped sharply. More than half reported popup irritation.

Spin Out already addresses part of this with cooldowns, family suppression, X-Ray routing and intentional silence.

## Spin Out decision
Keep priority order:
1. user-defined stopping-limit violations / acute urgent events
2. acute chasing / stake escalation / rapid uncontrolled loop
3. concrete real-life financial context when relevant
4. generic observations
5. silence when nothing earns interruption

Specific context may beat a generic observation when urgency is similar.

Do not:
- insert personal data into every Ping
- repeat the same bill/person frequently
- show personal context merely to demonstrate personalization
- increase popup frequency

Visual/content rule:
- one main sentence
- one supporting fact/question at most
- concrete amount/timing should be visually scannable
- explanation remains secondary

## Success criteria
- urgent chase/limit interventions still outrank ledger context
- concrete obligation/shortfall context outranks generic low/medium observations when both qualify
- family cooldowns remain
- one foreground surface remains
- no increase in Ping frequency
- strong intervention actions remain **Continue run** / **I'm done**
- contextual-Ping recall improves in synthetic panel without higher irritation
- perceived creepiness does not increase
- repeated use of the same sensitive field remains suppressed

## Sources
2025 rapid evidence assessment of responsible-gambling messages; randomized popup trial with recall/irritation measures; real-world personalized behavioral feedback studies; Swedish online-gambling personalized feedback experiment; personalized-feedback RCTs and gambling JITAI evidence; current consumer notification/insight patterns from WHOOP, Strava, Monzo/Revolut and Apple Health.
