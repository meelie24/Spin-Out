## Source freshness

The addenda provide exact URLs, publication/update dates where available, and a retrieval date of 19 September 2026. Undated official help is labeled as documentation observed on that date. A recent page update does not make an old illustration a new capture. Historic papers are used for bounded design principles; they are not evidence of current product interfaces.

Three exact reference images and six first-party comparison images from five products were inspected. [The image manifest](2026-09-19-product-image-manifest.json) preserves source URLs, timestamps and hashes. Full competitor account interactions remain unverified.

## Claims and limits

The papers support specific decisions about legibility, task boundaries, explanation, optional input and control. They do not establish Spin Out's clinical effectiveness, a universal voluntary-completion ceiling, conversion improvement, recall, or future-use intent.

The frozen synthetic method records real browser actions and state under declared scenario assumptions. WebChain, WebLINX and Mind2Web inform action/observation discipline. No fitted human calibration dataset was available, so the reports must describe deterministic synthetic stress tests and distinguish observed UI behavior from assumed motives and choices.

A 300-lens rubric organizes actual screenshot observations. It does not multiply one observer's finding into 300 independent votes. Source-observer counts, missing evidence and disagreements remain visible in the reports.
