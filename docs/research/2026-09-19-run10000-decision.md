# Research Decision — Run 10,000 Statistical Explanation

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Category:** Data visualization / simulation explanation

## Problem
Run 10,000 currently presents the important sample result and model expectation correctly, but then immediately places four additional statistics plus several explanatory lines on the same mobile surface.

## User signal
The 300-reviewer panel understood the concept but repeatedly flagged density, tiny supporting text, and competition between the two main numbers and secondary statistics.

## Relevant current products / patterns inspected
- Strava Fitness / Relative Effort
- Apple Health Trends
- Oura Trends
- WHOOP Recovery / impacts
- Monzo Trends
- Revolut Analytics
- Fidelity retirement analysis
- consumer financial forecast/simulation patterns from major retirement providers
- risk communication literature on probability, uncertainty and numeracy

## Evidence patterns
1. **Lead with the gist, then details on demand.** Consumer health/finance products show the current/important result first and let users drill down.
2. **Comparator labels must be explicit.** The audience must know what each number represents.
3. **Trends/ranges are often more useful than abstract raw model scores.**
4. **Uncertainty should be acknowledged in words as well as numbers.**
5. **Critical meaning should not live only in tiny text.**
6. **Low-numeracy users benefit from plain-language labels and clear comparisons; a complex graphic is not automatically easier.**

## Spin Out decision
Keep the two primary numbers visually dominant:
- **This 10,000-run sample**
- **Model expectation**

Immediately below them, show one plain-language interpretation that states whether the sample happened to finish above/below expectation and that one sample does not change the model.

Move:
- wins
- losses
- near misses
- longest losing stretch
- sample staked
- sample returned
- strategy note
- technical expectation basis

behind an explicit **See the numbers** / **Hide the numbers** control.

Do not hide the fact that the sample may finish ahead.

Do not imply certainty beyond the calibrated model.

## Success criteria
- sample result vs model expectation distinguished within seconds
- the two primary numbers are the loudest elements
- one plain-language interpretation visible without expansion
- no critical caveat exists only in small print
- secondary statistics remain one tap away
- no clipping at 320/390px
- focus/keyboard works
- reduced motion still jumps safely to final state
- synthetic users can correctly explain sample vs expectation after viewing
- 300-reviewer density/typography findings improve materially
